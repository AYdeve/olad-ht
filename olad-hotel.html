Our Story

The Heart of Olad

Nestled in the vibrant city of Akure, Hotel Olad was born from a vision — to create a haven where Nigerian warmth and world-class hospitality converge. Located on Oluwatuyi Street, Onu-Ara, we've become the preferred choice for discerning travellers across Ondo State and beyond.

We believe every guest deserves to feel at home — cared for, comfortable, and celebrated. Our dedicated team brings this belief to life every single day.

— The Olad Family
